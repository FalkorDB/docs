Unless otherwise noted, FalkorDB is dual-licensed under either the [Server Side Public License v1 (SSPLv1)](https://github.com/FalkorDB/FalkorDB/blob/main/LICENSE-SSPL) or the [GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3)](https://github.com/FalkorDB/FalkorDB/blob/main/LICENSE-AGPL), at your option. You may choose either license and must comply with its terms.

Third-party components may be subject to their own license terms and notices. See [Third-Party Libraries](/design/third-party) and the license files included with your distribution.

The dual-license notice and texts are included in container images at `/usr/local/share/licenses/falkordb/` and as release assets. Earlier releases may have different licensing terms; check the license files for the specific version you use.

## Licensing FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does dual licensing mean?">
    You can use FalkorDB under either SSPLv1 or AGPLv3, at your option. The two licenses have different conditions, so read the full text of the license you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do the licenses differ for network services?">
    SSPLv1 section 13 addresses making the functionality of the program available to third parties as a service and requires making the service source code available under its terms. AGPLv3 section 13 requires that users interacting with a modified version over a network have an opportunity to receive its corresponding source. Consult the license texts for the full requirements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use FalkorDB internally or commercially?">
    Both licenses permit internal and commercial use. Your obligations depend on how you use, modify, and distribute the software and on the license you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I modify or redistribute FalkorDB?">
    Both licenses permit modification and redistribution subject to their respective conditions, including source-code and license-notice requirements. Review the license you choose before distributing FalkorDB or making a modified version available to network users.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the same terms apply to FalkorDB SDKs and other libraries?">
    Not necessarily. Libraries and SDKs may have different licenses; check the license in each repository and the notices included with your distribution.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use an earlier FalkorDB release under AGPLv3?">
    Check the license files for that release. Earlier releases were published under their original SSPL terms and are not automatically covered by the dual-license notice for later versions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I have questions about my use case?">
    Contact the FalkorDB team to discuss licensing options, and consult your legal counsel for advice about your specific obligations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
