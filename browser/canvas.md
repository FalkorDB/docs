# FalkorDB Canvas

FalkorDB Canvas is the graph-rendering engine used by FalkorDB Browser.

- Repository: [FalkorDB/falkordb-canvas](https://github.com/FalkorDB/falkordb-canvas)
- Package: [@falkordb/canvas](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@falkordb/canvas)

## In Browser

Canvas powers:

- Node/edge rendering
- Pan/zoom and zoom-to-fit
- Layout modes (`force`, `tree`, `radial`)
- Selection and dragging interactions
- Viewport and style synchronization with Browser controls
